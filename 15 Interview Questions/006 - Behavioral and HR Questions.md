# Behavioral & HR Interview Questions

## 1. Definition

Structured collection of behavioral and HR-style interview questions designed to assess cultural fit, communication skills, leadership qualities, and past experiences.

## 2. Why do we need it?

Technical skills alone rarely secure offers — soft skills, teamwork, problem-solving, and emotional intelligence matter equally in hiring decisions.

## 3. Internal Working

Based on **STAR method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result):
1. Situation – Set the scene
2. Task – Define objective
3. Action – Explain specific steps taken
4. Result – Share measurable outcome

## 4. Step-by-Step Execution

Preparation workflow:
1. Reflect on personal experiences
2. Frame stories around achievements/challenges
3. Quantify results where possible
4. Practice concise storytelling

## 6. Examples (Easy → Advanced)

### Easy
**Q: Tell me about yourself.**
A: "I'm a Senior Frontend Developer with 5 years of experience building scalable web applications. Currently leading a team of 3 developers at XYZ Corp, focusing on Angular-based enterprise dashboards. Passionate about creating performant, accessible user interfaces that delight end-users."

### Medium
**Q: Describe a challenging project you led.**
A: "Led migration of legacy AngularJS app to Angular 17 with minimal downtime. Coordinated 4 engineers, implemented gradual rollout strategy cutting bundle size by 60% and improving load times 3x. Delivered 2 weeks ahead of schedule."

### Advanced
**Q: How do you handle disagreements in code reviews?**
A: "I focus on objective criteria — performance impact, maintainability, team conventions. When disagreeing, I ask probing questions rather than asserting opinions. Recently helped resolve architectural debate by prototyping two approaches side-by-side, letting data drive decision."

## 14. Interview Questions

Sample Behavioral Questions:

1. Tell me about yourself
2. Describe your strongest strength
3. What's your biggest weakness?
4. Tell me about a time you failed
5. Describe conflict resolution experience
6. How do you prioritize tasks?
7. Tell me about working under pressure
8. Describe mentoring/junior guidance
9. What motivates you?
10. Why do you want to join our company?

HR Questions:

1. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
2. Are you willing to relocate/travel?
3. What are your salary expectations?
4. Why are you leaving current job?
5. How do you handle feedback?

## 15. Follow-up Questions

- "Tell me more about that situation."
- "What would you do differently now?"

## 16. Production Best Practices

1. Prepare 3-5 compelling career stories
2. Quantify impact wherever possible
3. Practice clear concise delivery
4. Research company culture/values beforehand

## 17. Summary

Strong interpersonal narratives distinguish candidates beyond technical competence.

## 18. Revision Notes

- Have numbers/milestones ready
- Show growth from failures
- Align values with company mission
